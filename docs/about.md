# About

The Restructuring Newsletter is a weekly summary of developments in
restructuring and insolvency in the United Kingdom and the United States. It
covers two things: notable filings and deals, read from a finance point of
view, and short notes on the court decisions that shape how those deals get
done.

## Who it is for

Two groups at once, which shapes how every item is written.

**Professionals** — lawyers, advisers, insolvency practitioners and
investors — get the point first. Every case note opens with a bottom line of
two or three lines. Reading only those takes a couple of minutes.

**Students** get the same items with the doctrine explained. Each case note
carries a background paragraph, and every issue explains one concept from
first principles. Terms explained this way collect in the [glossary](glossary.md).

## How it is put together

Developments are gathered each week from public sources: SEC EDGAR filings,
CourtListener, the National Archives' Find Case Law service, the Insolvency
Service's statistics, and commentary from law firms and advisers.

Commentary is used to find developments and to check a reading of them. It is
never the cited authority for what a court held. **Every case note links to
the judgment itself**, so you can read the primary source rather than someone's
account of it. Paywalled sources are not cited, because students cannot reach
them.

A draft is assembled automatically and then read and edited by a person before
anything is published. Summaries of judgments are compressed by necessity, and
compression loses things; the link to the judgment is there for that reason.

## What it is not

This newsletter is published for information and education only. It is not
legal, financial or investment advice. It is not a substitute for advice on
any specific matter, and nothing in it creates a professional relationship of
any kind. Do not act on a summary. Read the decision, and take advice.

## Corrections

Corrections matter more here than speed. If something is wrong, please say so
by [opening an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/gattusopierre01-cloud/The-Restructuring-Newsletter/issues).
Corrections are made on the website and noted in the next issue.

## Privacy

If you subscribe by email, your address is held by the mail provider for the
purpose of sending you the newsletter and nothing else. It is not sold, shared
or used for anything other than this publication. Every issue carries an
unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing deletes the address.
